要在Ubuntu下安裝MongoDB，我們可以呼叫命令"sudo apt-get install mongodb"進行MongoDB的安裝。

![screenshot(132)_thumb.png](/images/posts/82d34706-b5ff-4f94-b6f8-767df2b87eb2/screenshot%28132%29_thumb.png)

![screenshot(133)_thumb.png](/images/posts/82d34706-b5ff-4f94-b6f8-767df2b87eb2/screenshot%28133%29_thumb.png)

安裝後呼叫命令"sudo service mongodb start"將MongoDB的服務啟動。

![screenshot(134)_thumb.png](/images/posts/82d34706-b5ff-4f94-b6f8-767df2b87eb2/screenshot%28134%29_thumb.png)

服務啟動後呼叫命令mongo，若可以正常的運行就表示安裝無誤。

![image_thumb.png](/images/posts/82d34706-b5ff-4f94-b6f8-767df2b87eb2/image_thumb.png)
